Before you book

Good to know.

Where do you offer mobile service?

I'm based in San Jose, California. Contact me with your location and the work you need so we can discuss whether a mobile visit is suitable.

Do the listed prices include parts?

No. The $40 oil change and tire rotation and $40 basic inspection are listed labor prices. Parts and materials are separate. Contact me to discuss your vehicle's requirements.

What should I send with my request?

Your vehicle's year, make, model, and the service or symptoms. If you're requesting a repair, describe what you've noticed and when it happens.

What if I don't know which service to choose?

Call, text, or send a message on Instagram. Describe what your car is doing so we can discuss a starting point. An OBD2 code scan can read stored codes; finding the cause may require further diagnosis.
